# HackLab fact sheet (single source of truth)

Every number or claim about HackLab in any article MUST come from this file.
If a fact is not here, do not state it. Do not invent quotes, testimonials,
names of participants, company names of hires, salaries, percentages, or process details.

## Organisation
- Name: HackLab (also written "HackLab Robotics"). Website: https://hacklabrobotics.com
- What it is: an organiser of AI × robotics (physical AI) hackathons in Berlin, Germany.
- Positioning: hackathons where companies meet and recruit pre-selected robotics, AI and ML builders
  by watching them build real prototypes under time pressure.
- Approved size claim (use exactly this framing, never "the largest" unqualified):
  "one of the largest physical AI hackathons in Berlin: 1,100+ registrations for 200 spots (Edition #2)".
  You may also say "Berlin's AI × robotics hackathon with 1,100+ registrations".
- Across its events HackLab has reached 1,500+ people (source: HackLab sponsor deck).

## Edition #1 — Berlin Robotics × Agentic AI Hackathon
- Dates: 20–21 June 2026. Venue: CIC Berlin. Format: 36 hours of continuous building.
- 600+ applications; 130 builders selected after reviewing every application.
- About 20 teams; close to 20 final solutions demoed to judges and sponsor companies.
- 5 participants were hired by sponsor companies after the event.
- 14 sponsor companies in total, including: Norma Core, Hugging Face, n8n, Lovable, Marso Robotics,
  Mistral AI, ITQ, Red Bull. (Do not name others as Edition #1 sponsors.)
- Who attended: robotics students finishing their degree, people with machine-learning backgrounds,
  robotics professionals across specialties, master's students building first real prototypes,
  university researchers.
- The first edition was bootstrapped, with no main partner covering costs from day one.
- Source: HackLab Edition #1 Impact Report.

## Edition #2 — AI × Robotics Hackathon II
- Dates: 21–22 November 2026. Venue: CIC Berlin, Lohmühlenstraße 65, Berlin. Format: 48 hours.
- Co-organised with BRISA (Berlin Robotics & Intelligent Systems Association).
- 1,100+ registrations (Luma); 200 builders selected. Applications are closed.
- €10,000+ in prizes (cash, hardware and more); free credits from every technology partner.
- Hardware on site: robot arms, robot sets and a Unitree Go2 EDU quadruped.
- Tracks: (1) AI Infrastructure, (2) Industrial Robotics Simulation, (3) Autonomous Navigation,
  (4) Wildcard / Open Source.
- Partners & technology providers: X Square Robot, Durance AI, Prior Labs, NormaCore, Hugging Face,
  Cognition, Anthropic, Nebius, Almedia, Seeed Studio, OpenELab, MyBotShop, Linkup, Red Bull.
- Event page: https://luma.com/h0mcaqio

## Value for companies (only these claims)
- Companies see candidates build working prototypes in 36–48 hours instead of reading CVs.
- Participants are pre-selected (Ed. #1: 130 of 600+; Ed. #2: 200 of 1,100+).
- Edition #1 produced 5 hires at sponsor companies.
- Sponsors can set challenges, provide hardware or credits, and meet builders in person.
