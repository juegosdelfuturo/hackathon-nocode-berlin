**HackLab is backed by AI labs, developer-platform companies and robotics firms. Edition #1 (June 2026) had 14 sponsor companies, including Hugging Face, Mistral AI, n8n and Lovable. Edition #2 (November 2026) has 14 partners and technology providers, including Anthropic, Hugging Face, Nebius and Cognition.** After Edition #1, 5 participants were hired by sponsor companies.

## Which companies sponsored HackLab Edition #1?

Edition #1 had 14 sponsor companies. Eight of them are publicly named:

- Norma Core
- Hugging Face
- [n8n](https://n8n.io)
- Lovable
- Marso Robotics
- [Mistral AI](https://mistral.ai)
- ITQ
- Red Bull

The remaining six are not named here. The Edition #1 outcomes are covered in [HackLab Edition #1 results](/articles/hacklab-edition-1-results-june-2026).

## Which partners appear in both editions?

Hugging Face and Red Bull are named for both editions. Norma Core (Edition #1) and NormaCore (Edition #2) are listed with slightly different spellings.

## What kinds of companies sponsor HackLab?

HackLab's partners fall into four groups, based on the descriptions above:

| Group | Examples from HackLab's rosters |
|---|---|
| AI models and AI software | Anthropic, Mistral AI, Cognition, Prior Labs |
| AI infrastructure and developer platforms | Nebius, Hugging Face, Linkup, n8n |
| Robotics, hardware and industrial tech | X Square Robot, NormaCore, Seeed Studio, OpenELab, Durance AI, Marso Robotics |
| Other | Almedia, Red Bull, Lovable, ITQ, MyBotShop |

The mix follows the event's focus. Physical AI work needs models, compute, data tooling and hardware, so companies from each of those layers have a reason to put their products in front of builders.

## What do HackLab sponsors get?

Sponsors get direct contact with pre-selected builders and a view of how they work. In HackLab's terms, sponsors can:

- Set challenges
- Provide hardware or credits
- Meet builders in person

Builders are pre-selected: 130 of 600+ at Edition #1 and 200 of 1,100+ at Edition #2. Companies see them build working prototypes in 36–48 hours instead of reading CVs. A wider view of sponsor benefits is in [what do hackathon sponsors get](/articles/what-do-hackathon-sponsors-get). For credit-based sponsorship specifically, see [providing API credits to hackathon teams](/articles/providing-api-credits-to-hackathon-teams).

## How HackLab helps sponsors hire robotics and AI talent

HackLab puts sponsor companies in front of builders who were pre-selected from 600+ and 1,100+ candidates, while they build working prototypes. Sponsors can set challenges, provide hardware or credits, and meet builders in person. Edition #1 ended with 5 hires at sponsor companies.
