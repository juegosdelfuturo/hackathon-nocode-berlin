**Berlin has over 130 robotics companies, according to Berlin Partner's Business Location Center, spread across industrial automation, care and health, and software and machine learning.** Most are small and specialised: AI vision for robot arms, inspection systems, cobots, fleet software for mobile robots, and robots for construction and textiles. The list below includes only companies whose own websites confirm a Berlin address.

For the hiring case behind these numbers, see [why Berlin for robotics and AI hiring](/articles/why-berlin-for-robotics-and-ai-hiring).

## How many robotics companies are in Berlin, and in which fields?

The [Business Location Center](https://businesslocationcenter.de/en/industry/robotik), Berlin's official economic development portal, states that Berlin has "over 130 robotics companies." It groups the sector into three main topic areas:

- **Industrial robotics and automation**
- **Care and health**
- **Software and machine learning**

The same page names the training routes that feed these companies:

- a bachelor's degree in humanoid robotics at the Berliner Hochschule für Technik (BHT),
- the master's "Industry 4.0: Automation, Robotics and 3D Manufacturing" at SRH Berlin University of Applied Sciences,
- the ABB training centre and dual apprenticeships in metal and electrical engineering.

It also lists support structures that companies can use: Berlin Partner's robotics networking meetings, the Robot News Berlin newsletter, the Competence Atlas Industry 4.0, the Motion.Lab Berlin prototyping space and the House of Robotics at BHT.

### AI software and vision

[micropsi industries](https://www.micropsi-industries.com/) sells MIRAI, AI-vision software that lets robots handle tasks "not viable with other systems." Its [imprint](https://www.micropsi-industries.com/imprint) lists an address on Lise-Meitner-Straße in Berlin.

[Gestalt Automation](https://gestalt-automation.com/), also known as Gestalt Robotics, builds AI automation for industrial inspection, serving pharma, food, manufacturing, energy and laboratory customers. Its [imprint](https://gestalt-automation.com/impressum) gives a Berlin address for Gestalt Automation GmbH.

[pi4_robotics](https://www.pi4.de/) works in machine vision and humanoid service robotics, with products, engineering and R&D projects. Its [about page](https://www.pi4.de/about.html) lists an address on Gustav-Meyer-Allee in Berlin.

### Robots for specific industries

[ConBotics](https://www.conbotics.com/) develops construction robots; its first product is a painting robot for walls and ceilings. Berlin Partner's [invest-in.berlin](https://www.invest-in.berlin/article/5-robots-from-berlin/) profile describes it as about 1.90 m tall and 130 kg, run from a floor plan entered in an app.

[ADOTC](https://adotc.de/en/index.html) develops automated cells and lines for textile manufacturing, centred on sewing, welding and bonding.

[KleRo](https://www.klero.de/), based on Siegfriedstraße in Berlin, automates recurring processes with mobile or stationary robots and covers concept, simulation, programming, integration and training.

### Hardware platforms and fleet software

[Continuum Innovation](https://www.continuum-innovation.de/) is registered in Berlin. Berlin Partner describes its AIRA arm as 2 m long, with a 4 kg moving part, lidar-based obstacle avoidance and a design for close work with people.

[Capandji](https://capandji.com/) makes tendon-driven robotic hands "hand-made in Berlin." The hand began as the SIRo Soft Hand at the Berliner Hochschule für Technik.

[SyncroBot](https://syncrobot.de/) provides fleet management for mixed AMR and AGV fleets in intralogistics, without requiring WMS or ERP integration.

## Do large companies run robotics work in Berlin too?

Yes, Berlin also has corporate robotics users. Berlin Partner [reports](https://invest-in.berlin/news/mercedes-embraces-ai-and-robotics-at-berlin-innovation-hub) that Mercedes-Benz runs its Digital Factory Campus in Berlin-Marienfelde, established in 2022 as its global centre for production digitalisation. The same report says Mercedes-Benz invested in Apptronik to bring the humanoid robot Apollo into manufacturing, first for intralogistics and quality checks.

## What does this mean for companies hiring in Berlin?

The startup landscape shapes the local talent pool in three ways:

1. **Skills skew to software and perception.** Several of the companies above sell AI and vision software rather than full robots, so local candidates often have perception, ML and integration experience.
2. **Applied domain knowledge is available.** Construction, textiles, logistics and inspection each have at least one Berlin company, so candidates with domain-specific robotics experience exist locally.
3. **Employers are small.** Many candidates come from small teams where roles overlap, from hardware bring-up to deployment. Test that breadth with practical tasks; see [how to hire robotics engineers in Berlin](/articles/how-to-hire-robotics-engineers-in-berlin).

## How can a company use this landscape for hiring and partnerships?

Map it before you recruit. A short process:

1. **List adjacent companies.** Note which Berlin firms work on problems close to yours, such as vision, manipulation or mobile-robot logistics.
2. **Identify shared skills.** Engineers at those companies have related domain experience, which tells you where your candidates are.
3. **Look for partners, not only hires.** A fleet-software provider, an integrator or a vision company can shorten your product timeline.
4. **Follow local channels.** The Business Location Center lists the Robot News Berlin newsletter and Robotics.Berlin research project updates.

## Where do Berlin robotics companies meet engineers?

They meet at network events, meetups and hackathons. The Business Location Center lists Berlin Partner's robotics networking meetings and the Berlin Robotics Meetup; more groups are covered in [Berlin's AI and robotics community](/articles/berlin-ai-and-robotics-community).

HackLab is the hands-on option. Edition #1 (June 2026, CIC Berlin) had 14 sponsor companies, and 5 participants were hired by sponsors afterwards.
