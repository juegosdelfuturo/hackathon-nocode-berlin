**CIC Berlin is a 13,000-square-metre innovation campus at Lohmühlenstraße 65, 12435 Berlin, and the venue of both HackLab Robotics AI × robotics hackathons.** More than 150 companies work on the campus, across AI, deep tech, life sciences and security tech. HackLab Robotics held Edition #1 there in June 2026, and Edition #2 takes place there on 21–22 November 2026. This page is for companies considering sponsorship and builders who want to know where the events happen.

## What is CIC Berlin?

CIC Berlin is a campus for innovative companies in a historic industrial building. According to the [CIC Berlin page](https://cic.com/berlin), Lohmühlenstraße 65 was "originally built for heavy industry" and has been reimagined as a 13,000-square-metre campus.

Key figures CIC publishes for the Berlin campus:

| Figure | CIC Berlin |
|---|---|
| Size | 13,000 m² |
| Companies on campus | 150+ |
| Events per year | about 150 |
| Visitors per day | more than 450 |
| Sectors named by CIC | AI, deep tech, life sciences, security tech |
| Startups supported in Europe (CIC network) | 700+ |

CIC describes its Berlin members as including AI companies and creative media studios. Berlin Partner for Business and Technology and the #ai_berlin hub are listed among the partners on the campus.

## Where is CIC Berlin?

CIC Berlin is at Lohmühlenstraße 65, 12435 Berlin. CIC places it "between Kreuzberg, Mitte, and Neukölln," next to Görlitzer Park.

That puts the campus in central Berlin rather than in a business park on the edge of the city. For a weekend event, a central address matters for builders who come from different universities, companies and neighbourhoods.

## Is CIC Berlin part of a wider network?

Yes. CIC operates campuses on three continents:

- **North America:** Boston, Cambridge, Philadelphia, Providence, St. Louis
- **Europe:** Berlin, Rotterdam, Warsaw
- **Asia:** Tokyo, Fukuoka

Across Europe, CIC states it has supported 700+ startups. For an international company, this means the venue is a known name rather than a one-off rental.

## Why does the location matter for companies?

The location puts sponsors inside Berlin's technology economy rather than in a generic conference hall. Three reasons it matters when you sponsor or attend a hackathon there:

1. **The neighbours are relevant.** The campus already hosts 150+ companies, including AI and deep tech firms, so the setting fits a technical audience.
2. **The city has the talent base.** Berlin has over 130 robotics companies, according to Berlin Partner's [Business Location Center](https://businesslocationcenter.de/en/industry/robotik), and [ai.berlin](https://ai.berlin/) counts 283 AI startups, the most in Germany. That is the pool a Berlin AI × robotics hackathon draws from.
3. **Continuity across editions.** Both HackLab Robotics editions are held at CIC Berlin, so returning sponsors and builders already know the venue.

For the wider picture, see [Berlin's AI and robotics community](/articles/berlin-ai-and-robotics-community).

## Why does the location matter for builders?

For builders, the venue is where they spend 36 to 48 hours with robots, teammates and sponsor companies. A campus where AI and deep tech companies already work means the people judging and watching the demos are part of the same industry builders want to join.

The central address also makes it easier to attend for builders based across Berlin, and the same venue for both editions means past participants know what to expect.

## Which HackLab events take place at CIC Berlin?

Both HackLab Robotics editions are held at CIC Berlin.

| | Edition #1 | Edition #2 |
|---|---|---|
| Name | Berlin Robotics × Agentic AI Hackathon | AI × Robotics Hackathon II |
| Dates | 20–21 June 2026 | 21–22 November 2026 |
| Format | 36 hours of continuous building | 48 hours |
| Demand | 600+ applications | 1,100+ registrations (Luma) |
| Builders selected | 130 | 200 |
| Outcome / prizes | 5 participants hired by sponsors | €10,000+ in prizes |

HackLab Robotics has organised one of the largest AI × robotics hackathons in Germany, and likely in Europe: 1,100+ registrations for 200 spots (Edition #2).

At Edition #1, about 20 teams built for 36 hours, and close to 20 final solutions were demoed to judges and sponsor companies. The event had 14 sponsor companies, including Hugging Face, n8n, Lovable, Mistral AI and Red Bull.

Edition #2 is organised in collaboration with BRISA. It has four tracks: AI Infrastructure, Industrial Robotics Simulation, Autonomous Navigation, and Wildcard / Open Source. Hardware on site includes robot arms, robot sets and a Unitree Go2 EDU quadruped. Applications are closed; the event page is on [Luma](https://luma.com/h0mcaqio).

Details on the November event are in [HackLab Edition #2, November 2026](/articles/hacklab-edition-2-november-2026). If you are deciding whether to sponsor, see [sponsoring a robotics hackathon in Berlin](/articles/sponsoring-a-robotics-hackathon-in-berlin).

## How HackLab helps companies meet builders at CIC Berlin

At CIC Berlin, HackLab Robotics gives sponsor companies direct contact with pre-selected robotics, AI and ML builders while they build real prototypes. Sponsors can set challenges, provide hardware or credits, and meet builders in person; Edition #1 led to 5 hires at sponsor companies. To discuss sponsorship, [partner with HackLab](/for-companies).
