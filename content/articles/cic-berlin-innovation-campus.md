**CIC Berlin is a 13,000-square-metre innovation campus at Lohmühlenstraße 65 in Berlin, set in the historic L65 complex originally built for heavy industry.** It hosts 150+ companies, about 150 events per year and more than 450 visitors per day. CIC Berlin is also the venue of both HackLab AI × robotics hackathons, including Edition #2 on 21–22 November 2026.

For companies, CIC Berlin is useful to know as a place to rent space, attend community events, and meet robotics and AI builders at HackLab.

## Where is CIC Berlin, and how do you get there?

CIC Berlin is at Lohmühlenstraße 65, 12435 Berlin, between Kreuzberg, Mitte and Neukölln, next to Görlitzer Park. The [CIC Berlin page](https://cic.com/berlin) lists these connections:

| Connection | Time |
|---|---|
| Bus 194 (Lohmühlenstraße stop) | 2-minute walk |
| Schlesisches Tor (U-Bahn) | 10-minute walk |
| Treptower Park (S-Bahn) | 15-minute walk |
| Warschauer Straße (S-Bahn and U-Bahn) | 20-minute walk |
| Berlin Ostbahnhof | 10-minute drive |
| Berlin Brandenburg Airport (BER) | 25-minute drive |

There is no car parking inside the building; street parking is possible nearby. The campus has a locked bike garage and an on-site shower room.

## What does CIC Berlin offer companies?

CIC Berlin offers workspace, event venues and a member community. Listed on CIC's site in November 2026:

- **Private offices and suites:** from €1,800 per month, for teams of 1 to 100+, furnished, with 24/7 access and unmetered meeting rooms.
- **Dedicated desks:** from €425 per month.
- **Coworking (hot desks):** from €250 per month.
- **Event space:** 13 event venues on site, including a Penthouse event space, an on-site cinema and media studio, and two inner courtyards.

CIC describes the campus as an alternative to traditional office districts such as Potsdamer Platz. Prices and terms change, so check the CIC page before you plan.

## Is CIC Berlin suitable for a robotics team?

It suits teams that need offices and meeting space more than a workshop. CIC lists lab space only for its Philadelphia and St. Louis campuses, not for Berlin. Robotics teams based there need a separate workshop for fabrication and heavy hardware.

Amenities that matter for engineering teams, as listed by CIC:

- Unmetered meeting rooms, phone booths and high-speed Wi-Fi
- 24/7 access for private offices
- Community kitchen, wellness and quiet zones, and a kids' room
- A pet-friendly policy with a designated pet-free area

For hardware prototyping space, makerspaces such as [MotionLab.Berlin](https://motionlab.berlin/) list prototyping labs and machinery.

Check contract terms before signing. CIC's marketing copy mentions flexible 30-day terms, while its FAQ states that terms range from month-to-month to typically 12–24 months, so confirm the term for your space in writing.

## Which communities meet at CIC Berlin?

Several Berlin innovation communities use the campus. The CIC page names these:

- **Venture Café Berlin**, which hosts weekly Thursday Gatherings at the campus to connect startups, investors and researchers. [Venture Café Berlin](https://venturecafeberlin.org/) lists them on Thursdays from 5:00 to 8:00 pm and describes its programming as free. Its current series runs weekly from Gathering #78 on 12 November to #82 on 10 December.
- **#ai_berlin hub**, listed by CIC among the partners on campus.
- **CIC programmes** such as International Connect Programs, a Japan Desk and Social Impact Cohorts.

CIC Berlin is part of a wider network. CIC lists campuses in Boston, Cambridge, Philadelphia, Providence and St. Louis in North America, Berlin, Rotterdam and Warsaw in Europe, and Tokyo and Fukuoka in Asia. Members can use other campuses through a "Global Passport." Across Europe, CIC states it has supported 700+ startups.

For more groups across the city, see [Berlin's AI and robotics community](/articles/berlin-ai-and-robotics-community).

## Which HackLab events take place at CIC Berlin?

Both HackLab editions are held at CIC Berlin.

| | Edition #1 | Edition #2 |
|---|---|---|
| Name | Berlin Robotics × Agentic AI Hackathon | AI × Robotics Hackathon II |
| Dates | 20–21 June 2026 | 21–22 November 2026 |
| Format | 36 hours of continuous building | 48 hours |
| Demand | 600+ applications | 1,100+ registrations (Luma) |
| Builders selected | 130 | 200 |
| Outcome / prizes | 5 participants hired by sponsors | €10,000+ in prizes |

At Edition #1, about 20 teams built for 36 hours, and close to 20 final solutions were demoed to judges and sponsor companies. The event had 14 sponsor companies, including Hugging Face, n8n, Lovable, Mistral AI and Red Bull.

Edition #2 is co-organised with BRISA (Berlin Robotics & Intelligent Systems Association). It has four tracks: AI Infrastructure, Industrial Robotics Simulation, Autonomous Navigation, and Wildcard / Open Source.

Hardware on site includes robot arms, robot sets and a Unitree Go2 EDU quadruped. Applications are closed.

Details on the November event are in [HackLab Edition #2, November 2026](/articles/hacklab-edition-2-november-2026).

## What should sponsor companies know before visiting CIC Berlin?

Plan logistics around the building's location and access. A short checklist for sponsor teams:

1. **Travel:** from BER, allow about 25 minutes by car; by public transport, Schlesisches Tor and Treptower Park are the nearest rail stations.
2. **Hardware delivery:** there is no parking inside the building, so plan drop-off and street parking for any robots or equipment you bring.
3. **Team presence:** HackLab's format is continuous building over 36–48 hours, so schedule engineers and recruiters across the event rather than for a single slot.
4. **Follow-on meetings:** weekly Venture Café Thursday Gatherings on the same campus are an option for meeting startups and researchers outside the hackathon.

If you are deciding whether to sponsor, see [sponsoring a robotics hackathon in Berlin](/articles/sponsoring-a-robotics-hackathon-in-berlin).

At CIC Berlin, HackLab gives sponsor companies direct contact with pre-selected robotics, AI and ML builders while they build real prototypes. Sponsors can set challenges, provide hardware or credits, and meet builders in person; Edition #1 led to 5 hires at sponsor companies. To discuss sponsorship, [partner with HackLab](/for-companies).
