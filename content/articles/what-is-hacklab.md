**HackLab is an organiser of AI × robotics (physical AI) hackathons in Berlin, Germany, where companies meet and recruit pre-selected robotics, AI and ML builders by watching them build real prototypes under time pressure.** It ran Edition #1, a 36-hour event, at CIC Berlin in June 2026, and runs Edition #2, a 48-hour event, there on 21–22 November 2026. It is one of the largest physical AI hackathons in Berlin: 1,100+ registrations for 200 spots (Edition #2).

The name is also written "HackLab Robotics". The website is [hacklabrobotics.com](https://hacklabrobotics.com).

## What happens at a HackLab hackathon?

Pre-selected builders form teams and build working robotics and AI prototypes over 36–48 hours. At the end, they demo their solutions to judges and sponsor companies. At Edition #1, about 20 teams demoed close to 20 final solutions.

The work happens on real hardware. Edition #2 provides robot arms, robot sets and a Unitree Go2 EDU quadruped on site. It also runs four tracks:

1. AI Infrastructure
2. Industrial Robotics Simulation
3. Autonomous Navigation
4. Wildcard / Open Source

Each track is explained in [HackLab Edition #2 tracks explained](/articles/hacklab-edition-2-tracks-explained).

## Who takes part in HackLab?

HackLab participants are selected builders with robotics, AI or ML backgrounds. Edition #1 drew five main profiles:

- Robotics students finishing their degree
- People with machine-learning backgrounds
- Robotics professionals across specialties
- Master's students building their first real prototypes
- University researchers

Selection is competitive. Edition #1 accepted 130 builders from 600+ applications after reviewing every application, and Edition #2 accepted 200 from 1,100+ registrations. Profiles are described in more detail in [who attends HackLab](/articles/who-attends-hacklab).

## Why do companies work with HackLab?

Companies work with HackLab to see candidates build before they hire. Instead of reading CVs, a company watches engineers build a working prototype in 36–48 hours.

What HackLab offers companies:

- **Pre-selected talent.** 130 of 600+ applicants at Edition #1, and 200 of 1,100+ registrants at Edition #2.
- **Evidence of skill.** Working prototypes built under time pressure, demoed to sponsors.
- **A track record of hires.** Edition #1 led to 5 hires at sponsor companies.
- **Direct involvement.** Sponsors can set challenges, provide hardware or credits, and meet builders in person.

## Which companies have partnered with HackLab?

HackLab has worked with AI labs, developer-tool companies and robotics firms. Edition #1 had 14 sponsor companies, including Norma Core, Hugging Face, n8n, Lovable, Marso Robotics, Mistral AI, ITQ and Red Bull.

Edition #2 partners and technology providers are X Square Robot, Durance AI, Prior Labs, NormaCore, Hugging Face, Cognition, Anthropic, Nebius, Almedia, Seeed Studio, OpenELab, MyBotShop, Linkup and Red Bull. Every technology partner gives participants free credits. The full roster is in [HackLab partners and sponsors](/articles/hacklab-partners-and-sponsors).

## Where is HackLab held?

Both editions take place at CIC Berlin, an innovation campus at Lohmühlenstraße 65 in Berlin. [CIC describes the building](https://cic.com/berlin) as a former heavy-industry site converted into a 13,000-square-meter campus that more than 150 companies call home. More on the venue is in [CIC Berlin innovation campus](/articles/cic-berlin-innovation-campus).

## How did HackLab start?

HackLab Robotics started with its first edition in June 2026. It drew 600+ applications and ended with 5 hires at sponsor companies.

The second edition is organised in collaboration with BRISA. Edition #2 drew 1,100+ registrations for 200 spots.

## How HackLab helps companies hire robotics and AI talent

HackLab puts companies in the same room as pre-selected robotics, AI and ML builders while they build working prototypes. Sponsors can set challenges, provide hardware or credits, and meet builders in person. After Edition #1, 5 participants were hired by sponsor companies.
