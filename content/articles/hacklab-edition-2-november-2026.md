**HackLab Edition #2, the AI × Robotics Hackathon II, takes place on 21–22 November 2026 at CIC Berlin, Lohmühlenstraße 65. It is a 48-hour event, co-organised with BRISA, for 200 builders selected from 1,100+ registrations.** Applications are closed. Teams build across four tracks for €10,000+ in prizes, using robot arms, robot sets and a Unitree Go2 EDU quadruped on site.

## How competitive was selection for Edition #2?

Selection was competitive: 1,100+ people registered for 200 spots. On that basis, HackLab is one of the largest physical AI hackathons in Berlin: 1,100+ registrations for 200 spots (Edition #2).

For comparison, Edition #1 accepted 130 builders from 600+ applications. Edition #2 seats 70 more builders and runs 12 hours longer (48 hours instead of 36). The exact selection criteria are not covered here.

## What will teams build at Edition #2?

Teams build robotics and AI prototypes in one of four tracks:

1. **AI Infrastructure**
2. **Industrial Robotics Simulation**
3. **Autonomous Navigation**
4. **Wildcard / Open Source**

Each track is explained, with the skills it points to, in [HackLab Edition #2 tracks explained](/articles/hacklab-edition-2-tracks-explained).

## What hardware and resources are on site?

Builders work with real hardware, not only simulators. On site:

- Robot arms
- Robot sets
- A Unitree Go2 EDU quadruped

On top of the hardware, every technology partner gives participants free credits. Companies thinking about providing equipment to future events can read [providing hardware to a robotics hackathon](/articles/providing-hardware-to-a-robotics-hackathon).

## What does BRISA's role add?

BRISA co-organises Edition #2 with HackLab. On [its website](https://brisa-robotics.de), BRISA describes itself as a Berlin-wide, student-led robotics association connecting talent, research and industry. The partnership is covered in [HackLab and BRISA](/articles/hacklab-and-brisa).

## How is Edition #2 different from Edition #1?

Edition #2 is larger, longer and co-organised. Both editions are held at CIC Berlin.

| | Edition #1 (June 2026) | Edition #2 (November 2026) |
|---|---|---|
| Duration | 36 hours | 48 hours |
| Demand | 600+ applications | 1,100+ registrations |
| Selected | 130 | 200 |
| Organisation | Bootstrapped | Co-organised with BRISA |
| Tracks | Not stated | 4 tracks |
| Prizes | Not stated | €10,000+ |

Edition #1 ended with 5 hires at sponsor companies. Edition #2 has not taken place yet, so it has no outcomes to report.

## Can companies still get involved in Edition #2?

Applications for builders are closed. Companies that want to work with HackLab, at this edition or a future one, can get in touch through the for-companies page. Dates and location for a third edition are not covered here.

## How HackLab helps companies meet AI and robotics builders

HackLab gives companies two days in the same room as 200 builders who were pre-selected from 1,100+ registrations, watching them build working prototypes on real hardware. Sponsors can set challenges, provide hardware or credits, and meet builders in person. To discuss involvement, [partner with HackLab](/for-companies).
