**Germany is Europe's largest robot market: it installed about 24,800 industrial robots in 2025, 41% of all installations in the EU and the fifth-highest total worldwide.** Its robot density of 449 robots per 10,000 manufacturing employees (2024 data) ranks third in the world. Installations fell 8% in 2025, while the global market grew 11%.

These figures come from the International Federation of Robotics (IFR) and the VDMA, Germany's machinery association. They describe the market that robotics engineers in Germany work in.

## How many industrial robots does Germany install each year?

Germany installed fewer than 25,000 industrial robots in 2025, down 8%, according to the IFR's [September 2026 release](https://ifr.org/ifr-press-releases/news/five-million-robots-now-operate-in-factories-globally). The [World Robotics 2026 summary](https://ifr.org/worldrobotics/report-2026) puts the figure at 24,800 units.

This follows 26,982 units in 2024, a 5% fall that the IFR's [World Robotics 2025 release](https://ifr.org/ifr-press-releases/news/global-robot-demand-in-factories-doubles-over-10-years) called the second-best result after the record year 2023. Over 2020–2025, German installations still grew by an average of 2% per year.

## How does Germany compare with other robot markets?

Germany is fifth worldwide by installations. The IFR's 2025 ranking:

| Rank | Country | Installations 2025 | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | China | 354,000 | +20% |
| 2 | United States | ~38,500 | +12% |
| 3 | Japan | 36,219 | −19% |
| 4 | Republic of Korea | ~30,000 | −1% |
| 5 | Germany | ~24,800 | −8% |
| 6 | India | ~10,500 | +15% |

Globally, more than 600,000 robots were installed in 2025 (+11%), and the operational stock reached a record 5 million units. The IFR forecasts 655,000 installations in 2026.

Within Europe, Germany is ahead of Italy (about 7,800 units), France (about 4,500) and Spain (about 4,300). The EU-27 passed 700,000 operational industrial robots in 2025.

## What share of Europe's robots does Germany account for?

Germany accounted for 41% of industrial robot installations in the EU in 2025, according to the World Robotics 2026 summary. The IFR's previous report, which measured against Europe as a whole (a wider base than the EU), put Germany's 2024 share at about 32%.

Europe as a whole is growing more slowly than Asia and the Americas. The World Robotics 2026 summary says Europe took 12% of new industrial robot deployments in 2025, and that the EU-27 holds 84% of the robots in operation across Europe.

For employers, this means Germany remains Europe's largest single market for industrial robots, even in a year of falling installations.

## How automated is German manufacturing?

Germany has 449 industrial robots per 10,000 manufacturing employees, third in the world. The figure is from the IFR's [April 2026 robot density release](https://ifr.org/downloads/press_docs/DE-2026-APRIL-_8-IFR_PRESSMELDUNG_Roboterdichte-deutsch.pdf), based on 2024 data in World Robotics 2025.

- **Republic of Korea:** 1,220
- **Singapore:** 818
- **Germany:** 449, growing 5% per year since 2019
- **Japan:** 446
- **United States:** 307
- **EU-27 average:** 231
- **World average:** 132

## Which industries drive robot demand in Germany?

Automotive and metal are the two largest customers. The World Robotics 2026 summary attributes 23% of German demand to the automotive sector and 21% to the metal industry.

## How big is Germany's robotics and automation industry?

The [VDMA Robotics + Automation](https://vdma.eu/en-GB/robotics-automation) association represents more than 400 member companies across robotics, machine vision and automated assembly and handling solutions. It expects the industry's revenue to fall 5% to €14.1 billion in 2026 and describes conditions as challenging.

## What about service robots and humanoids?

Service robotics is growing faster than industrial robotics, but the IFR reports it globally rather than by country. Its [service robot release](https://ifr.org/ifr-press-releases/news/global-sales-of-professional-service-robots-surge-24-percent) for 2025 shows:

- Professional service robot shipments up 24% to almost 250,000 units.
- Transportation and logistics as the largest application, with 117,500 units (47% share).
- Surgical robots at 8,400 units, up 57%.

The World Robotics 2026 summary adds that about 7,000 full-size humanoids (above 140 cm) were sold globally in 2025, mostly for specialised tasks that often still need human teleoperation.

## Where does robotics research and industry meet in Germany?

National research is coordinated through the Robotics Institute Germany (RIG). The [University of Bonn's announcement](https://uni-bonn.de/en/news/robotics-institute-germany-pools-top-level-research) describes it as a consortium of 14 core institutions led by TU Munich, including TU Berlin, KIT, RWTH Aachen, DLR and DFKI, funded with €20 million by the Federal Ministry of Education and Research from July 2024 for four years.

## What do these numbers mean for companies hiring robotics engineers?

They point to steady demand for integration and automation skills, despite falling installations. Three takeaways:

1. **A large installed base needs people.** Germany's stock of robots in automotive and metal plants requires engineers to integrate, maintain and upgrade systems.
2. **Growth is shifting.** Service robots and logistics are growing globally while German industrial installations decline, so candidates who combine robotics with AI, perception and mobile systems fit the growing segments.
3. **Sourcing needs more than job ads.** With industry revenue expected to fall in 2026, companies that keep hiring need channels that show candidates' practical skills.
