**HackLab co-organises its second hackathon, the AI × Robotics Hackathon II (21–22 November 2026, CIC Berlin), with BRISA, the Berlin Robotics & Intelligent Systems Association.** On [its website](https://brisa-robotics.de), BRISA describes itself as a Berlin-wide, student-led robotics association connecting talent, research and industry. For companies, the partnership links HackLab's hackathon to an organised student robotics community in the city.

## What is BRISA?

BRISA is a student-led robotics association based in Berlin. Its website describes it as "Berlin-wide" and gives its mission as "Making Berlin a leading hub for Physical AI." On its Luma event pages, it uses the tagline "Building the future of Physical AI in Berlin."

BRISA's site names three focus areas: **Build**, **Research** and **Connect**. It also says:

- It takes on ambitious robotics projects that span several engineering disciplines, chosen to push members beyond their current skills.
- Students can apply to form teams that explore hard robotics problems through engineering, experimentation and research.
- It is looking for companies, universities, labs and institutions as partners.

BRISA's website says it is for students and early-career engineers who want hands-on robotics work. It also lists an advisory circle that includes professors from TU Berlin robotics labs and an investor.

## What does BRISA run?

Besides the hackathon, BRISA runs a series of robotics reading groups. Its event pages describe them as a "pre-launch series" ahead of an "official launch this fall".

The reading groups discuss recent robotics and physical AI research papers. One example is the [LeWorldModel session on Luma](https://luma.com/70uyhx31), hosted at Merantix AI Campus in Berlin. BRISA's site lists PROTOTYPE Capital and Merantix AI Campus as supporters.

## Where do BRISA's events take place?

BRISA's reading groups and the HackLab hackathon use different Berlin venues:

| Activity | Venue |
|---|---|
| BRISA reading groups (examples on Luma) | Merantix AI Campus, Max-Urich-Straße 3, 13355 Berlin |
| HackLab × BRISA hackathon (Edition #2) | CIC Berlin, Lohmühlenstraße 65, Berlin |

Both sit inside Berlin's wider AI and robotics scene. More about the hackathon venue is in [CIC Berlin innovation campus](/articles/cic-berlin-innovation-campus).

## What does the HackLab × BRISA partnership cover?

The partnership covers HackLab Edition #2. HackLab and BRISA co-organise the AI × Robotics Hackathon II.

| Edition #2 detail | Value |

| Dates | 21–22 November 2026 |
| Venue | CIC Berlin, Lohmühlenstraße 65, Berlin |
| Format | 48 hours |
| Registrations | 1,100+ (Luma) |

HackLab's first edition in June 2026 was bootstrapped, with no main partner covering costs. Edition #2 is co-organised with BRISA. Full logistics are in [HackLab Edition #2](/articles/hacklab-edition-2-november-2026).

## Why does a student-led robotics association matter to companies?

A student-led association is a direct route to robotics talent before graduation. HackLab's Edition #1 participants already included robotics students finishing their degree and master's students building their first real prototypes. BRISA describes its own audience as students and early-career engineers who want hands-on robotics work.

For a hiring company, this means:

- **Earlier access.** Students who build robots outside their coursework are visible before they reach the job market.
- **Visible interest.** Joining project teams or reading groups is voluntary and happens outside coursework.

Other parts of the city's ecosystem are covered in [Berlin AI and robotics community](/articles/berlin-ai-and-robotics-community) and [robotics research institutions in Berlin](/articles/robotics-research-institutions-in-berlin).

## How can companies work with HackLab and BRISA?

Companies can work with each organisation directly. For the hackathon, HackLab's sponsors can set challenges, provide hardware or credits, and meet builders in person. For BRISA's own projects and reading groups, its website lists contact@brisa-robotics.de for partnership inquiries.

## How HackLab helps companies hire robotics talent with BRISA

Through Edition #2, HackLab and BRISA bring 200 builders, selected from 1,100+ registrations, to CIC Berlin for 48 hours of building on real hardware. Companies see robotics and AI candidates prove their skills in working prototypes, not on paper. After HackLab's first edition, 5 participants were hired by sponsor companies.
