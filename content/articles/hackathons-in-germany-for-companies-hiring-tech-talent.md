HackLab ranks first because it is the only event on this list whose published materials report a hiring result: 5 hires at sponsor companies after Edition #1.

## What does each hackathon offer a hiring team?

Each event gives companies a different kind of access. The notes focus on what matters for recruiting: audience, format and the partner's role.

### 1. HackLab (Berlin)

HackLab is built for companies that recruit robotics, AI and ML builders, such as robotics students finishing their degree, master's students building first real prototypes, people with machine-learning backgrounds, robotics professionals and university researchers. After Edition #1 (June 2026), 5 participants were hired by sponsor companies. Edition #2 runs 21–22 November 2026 at CIC Berlin.

### 2. hackaTUM (Garching near Munich)

[hackaTUM](https://hack.tum.de/) is the official hackathon of TUM's Department of Computer Science, held every year since 2016. Industry partners offer challenges and workshops, and the 2026 sponsor list includes CHECK24, Siemens and Optiver. The 2026 edition runs 27–29 November, and [TUM's event page](https://www.tum.de/en/news-and-events/events/details/hackatum-for-computer-science-and-innovation) names students as the target audience.

### 3. TUM.ai Makeathon (Munich)

The [TUM.ai Makeathon](https://makeathon.tum-ai.com/) is a 3-day hackathon on TUM's campus for students and young professionals. Teams build AI solutions for business cases presented by industry partners. The page reports more than 1,200 participants across past editions, with previous sponsors including Allianz, BMW, Microsoft and Roche.

### 4. robo.innovate (Munich and Garmisch-Partenkirchen)

[robo.innovate](https://roboinnovate.mirmi.tum.de/hackathon/) is a six-day robotics hackathon from TUM robo.innovate, TUM Venture Lab Robotics / AI and UnternehmerTUM MakerSpace. Full attendance is required across the six days, and partners include NEURA Robotics, Doosan and Franka Robotics.

### 5. Europe Embodied Hackathon (Freising)

The [Europe Embodied Hackathon](https://luma.com/ee26-hack) was the 48-hour hardware challenge of Robotics Week Munich 2026. It took 100 participants in teams of 3–4, and final presentations took place on the Ecosystem Summit main stage. Partners included RoboTUM, START Munich and the EIT Higher Education Initiative.

### 6. BCX26 (Berlin)

[BCX26](https://bosch-connected-world.com/bcx26/) is Bosch's hackathon: around 100 selected participants, 20 hours and four tracks covering mobility, manufacturing, advanced robotics and cognitive platforms. Challenges were announced before the event, and applicants had to apply for a spot.

### 7. Big Berlin Hack (Berlin)

[Big Berlin Hack](https://luma.com/bigberlinhack) is a weekend AI hackathon hosted by {Tech: Europe}, CODE University and The Delta Campus. Its listing title claims more than 300 builders and more than 50k in prizes. It suits companies that want broad visibility among Berlin's AI builders.

### 8. Cursor AI-Hackathon (Hamburg)

The [Cursor AI-Hackathon Hamburg](https://luma.com/hl7wv7k3) ran 31 January – 1 February 2026 at Bucerius Law School. Its organisers describe it as "the biggest AI hackathon in Germany's history (400+ participants)", and the page lists 561 people as having attended. It was open to non-technical participants, so the audience is broader than an engineering-only event.

### 9. HackHPI (Potsdam)

[HackHPI](https://hackhpi.org/) is a student hackathon on HPI's campus in Potsdam. The 2026 edition ran 10–11 April as an all-night event focused on agriculture, climate and tech.

## How do German hackathons select participants, and why does it matter?

Selection decides how much screening a company has to do on site. Curated events hand you a pre-filtered group; open events give you reach but leave the filtering to you.

- **Application review:** HackLab reviewed every application for Edition #1 (130 selected from 600+). BCX26 and the TUM.ai Makeathon also review applications.
- **Caps:** the Europe Embodied Hackathon took 100 participants.
- **Open or broad access:** events such as the Cursor AI-Hackathon welcome non-technical participants.

## What should a company do after a German hackathon?

Move fast and keep it personal. A short, consistent follow-up turns contacts into interviews:

1. **Within 48 hours:** message the builders you watched, referencing what they built.
2. **Within a week:** book technical conversations based on the prototype, not a generic screen.
3. **For international candidates:** check visa routes early. See [hiring international engineers in Germany](/articles/hiring-international-engineers-in-germany).
